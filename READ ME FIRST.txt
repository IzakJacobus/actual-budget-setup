ACTUAL BUDGET - QUICK GUIDE
===========================

Your own private budget app, on your laptop and your phone.
It starts by itself, backs itself up every day, and only YOUR devices can reach it.

You need: a Windows 11 laptop, a phone, and about 15 minutes.


INSTALL (on the laptop)
-----------------------
1. Double-click  Install.cmd  and click Yes when Windows asks.

2. Type  y  and press Enter when it offers to install Node.js and Tailscale.

3. Sign in to Tailscale when asked:
   - Click the Tailscale icon near the clock (you may need the ^ arrow first).
   - Choose "Log in" and make a free account.

4. Turn on HTTPS when asked:
   - Open  https://login.tailscale.com/admin/dns
   - Click "Enable HTTPS".
   - Go back to the window and press Enter.

5. Write down the phone address it shows at the end.
   It is also saved in  C:\actual-server\PHONE-URL.txt


FIRST USE
---------
1. On the laptop, open  http://localhost:5006  in your browser.
2. Create your password. Choose it yourself and write it down.
3. Click "Create new file" to start a budget.
   Or, to bring in an existing budget, use "Import file" and then "Actual".


ON YOUR PHONE
-------------
1. Install the Tailscale app. Sign in with the SAME account and switch it on.
2. Open your phone address:
   - iPhone: in Safari
   - Android: in Chrome
3. Add it to your home screen so it works like an app:
   - iPhone: tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen"
   - Android: tap the three dots, then "Install app"


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- The laptop must be on and signed in for the phone to sync.
  While it's off, the phone still works and syncs later.
- Your budget is kept in       C:\actual-server\data
- Backups are kept in          C:\actual-server\backups
  One is made every day and each time the laptop starts.
  Backups from the last 30 days are kept (and never fewer than 30).
- IMPORTANT: those backups are on the same laptop. If the laptop is lost or its
  disk dies, the backups go with it. To keep a second copy somewhere safe,
  open  C:\actual-server\settings.json  in Notepad and fill in "extraBackupDir":
      "extraBackupDir": "C:\\Users\\YOURNAME\\OneDrive\\Actual backups",
  (use double backslashes). A OneDrive, Google Drive or USB folder works.
- Encryption is optional (in your budget: Settings, then Encryption).
  If you forget the encryption password, your budget CANNOT be recovered.


UNINSTALL
---------
Double-click  Uninstall.cmd  and press Y.
This stops the app, removes its automatic start and backups, and turns off phone access.
Your budget data and backups are NOT deleted.
To remove everything, delete the folder  C:\actual-server  afterwards,
but only once you are sure you no longer need your budget.
